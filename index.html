Research

M.Sc. thesis: row orientation and row arrangement in maize-soybean intercropping

Advisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Harun Or Rashid, Department of Agronomy, BAU

The thesis asks how the direction and layout of crop rows change the way maize and soybean share light, and what that means for yield.

  • Tests combinations of row orientation and row arrangement at different inter-row spacings.
  • Measures light (PAR) at several canopy depths and chlorophyll (SPAD) in both crops.
  • Links light differences from row orientation to leaf nitrogen and yield.

Across my work, the common thread is getting more from limited land, water and fertilizer on small farms.
